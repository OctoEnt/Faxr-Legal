Delete Your Faxr Account

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

You can delete your Faxr account and its associated data at any time, either in the app or by contacting us. Faxr is operated by Octo Ent LLC.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Faxr and sign in.
  2. Go to Account (the profile icon).
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email help@octoent.com from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Faxr account deletion". We will confirm and complete your request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Apple, Google, or Discord login)
  • Your saved fax recipients (address book)
  • Notification settings and device notification tokens

Any active subscription is cancelled in Faxr. App Store and Google Play subscriptions are billed by Apple and Google; cancel them in your store account settings to stop future charges.

What is kept, and why

  • Fax records (sent and received fax details and documents) and purchase records (credit and subscription transactions) are retained as required for legal, tax, and accounting obligations.
  • A minimal record of the deleted account, linked to those records, is kept for the same purposes.

If you want us to delete retained fax documents as well, say so in your email and we will remove them unless the law requires us to keep them.
